Privacy Policy

Pocket Trainer (personal) is a private application built and used by a single person, its owner, to keep a personal fitness log.

What the app reads

With the owner's own authorization, the app reads the owner's sleep, readiness and related daily summary data from the Oura API.

What it does with the data

The data is copied into the owner's private Notion workspace. It is not sold, shared, published, or given to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or analytics.

Storage

Access credentials are stored encrypted in the owner's own Cloudflare account. The owner can revoke access at any time from the Oura account settings.

Other users

The app has no other users and collects no data from anyone but its owner.
